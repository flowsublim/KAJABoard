# KAJABoard Phase 4 Completion Gate

Phase 4 establishes explicit five-treatment purchasing classification, PO commitment, INTERNAL/SUBCONTRACT SPK planning, partial subcontract dispatch/acceptance, Warehouse candidate contracts, payable-source evidence, overhead-source evidence, Project committed cost, vendor analytics summary/detail, and modal previews for PO, SPK, Kirim Bahan, and Terima Maklun.

It does not create Warehouse movements, AP, journals, payments, WIP, or final HPP. Shared JASA_UMUM remains unallocated until the future HPP allocation gate.

The gate is verified by the Phase 4 regression suite, including print/modal and Vendor Analytics scope checks.
